About AnalyticsAdda

What this site is, who it is for, and the principles behind how the tutorials are written.

What this is#

AnalyticsAdda is a free, structured learning portal for business analytics. It covers the ground a working analyst is expected to know: framing problems, statistics, SQL, visualization, predictive modelling, and how analytics is practised inside marketing, finance, supply chain and HR functions.

There is no sign-up, no paywall and no course to buy. Every tutorial is a full page you can read, print or share.

Who it is for#

  • Students preparing for analytics roles or coursework.
  • Career switchers moving into analytics from a business, finance or operations background.
  • Working analysts who want to shore up specific topics — window functions, experiment design, CLV.
  • Managers who commission analyses and want to ask better questions of them.

The only assumption is comfort with a spreadsheet. SQL and Python are taught from the beginning where they appear.

How tutorials are written#

Every tutorial follows the same shape, because knowing what to expect makes learning faster:

  1. The concept in plain English, before any notation.
  2. The formula or syntax, with each term explained.
  3. A worked example on realistic business data, with numbers you can follow.
  4. The mistakes people actually make, called out explicitly.
  5. Key takeaways and a short quiz.

Three editorial principles guide the content:

Business context always. Every example is a decision somebody has to make. A statistical technique with no decision attached is a maths exercise, and there are better places to learn maths.

Honest about limitations. Where a method fails, the tutorial says so. Where a common practice is questionable — MAPE, feature importance as causation, attribution modelling — that is stated plainly rather than glossed over.

Runnable code. SQL and Python examples are written to be pasted into a console and adapted, not admired.

Progress tracking#

Marking a tutorial complete stores that state in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent anywhere, there is no account, and clearing your browser data resets it. Track progress per track on the tracks page.

Corrections#

Analytics content ages and mistakes happen. If something here is wrong, unclear or out of date, corrections are genuinely welcome — see Contribute.

Technical notes#

The site is a static site generated from Markdown by a small Node script with no third-party dependencies. Search runs entirely in your browser against a pre-built index; there is no tracking, no analytics script and no cookies. It works offline once loaded.
