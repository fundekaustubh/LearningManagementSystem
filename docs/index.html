monthly_revenue.sql
SELECT date_trunc('month', order_date) AS mth,
       sum(revenue)              AS revenue,
       sum(revenue) / lag(sum(revenue))
         OVER (ORDER BY 1) - 1 AS mom_growth
FROM   orders
WHERE  status = 'completed'
GROUP BY 1
ORDER BY 1;
Month-over-month growth in six lines — covered in Window Functions.

How this site is organised

Every tutorial follows the same shape so you always know what you are getting: the concept in plain English, the formula or syntax, a worked example on realistic business data, the mistakes people actually make, and a short quiz.

  • Concept first. No jargon before it has been defined.
  • Business context always. Every example is a decision someone has to make.
  • Runnable code. SQL and Python you can paste into a console.
  • Progress tracking. Mark tutorials complete; progress is saved in your browser.
